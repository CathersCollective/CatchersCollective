# The Catcher's Collective

Static first-version website for Josh Crouch's catcher training brand.

## Files

- `index.html` - Main landing page for catcher training in Murfreesboro and Franklin.
- `idlife.html` - Fitness, nutrition, and IDLife product shell.
- `styles.css` - Site-wide responsive styling.
- `script.js` - Sticky header state and temporary form behavior.

## Notes

- The contact form is a front-end placeholder and should be connected to the final CRM, booking tool, or email endpoint.
- The IDLife product buttons are placeholders until Josh's associate microsite links are available.
- Public Josh Crouch references are linked to MiLB and MLB sources from the site.
